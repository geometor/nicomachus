geometor.nicomachus.ten_means_and_cube
======================================

.. py:module:: geometor.nicomachus.ten_means_and_cube

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   Ten Nicomachean Proportions & the Cube Harmony 6 : 8 :: 9 : 12 (Book II, Chs. 21–29).

   Formalizes the culminating chapters of Nicomachus of Gerasa's *Introduction to Arithmetic*:

   - **All Ten Means** (*δέκα μεσότητες*, II.21–28):

     - The three classical archetypes from Pythagoras, Archytas, Plato, and Aristotle:
       **1. Arithmetic**, **2. Geometric**, **3. Harmonic**.
     - The three subcontrary proportions (*ὑπεναντίαι*): **4. Subcontrary to Harmonic**,
       **5. First Subcontrary to Geometric**, **6. Second Subcontrary to Geometric**.
     - The four later Neo-Pythagorean proportions (*τέσσαρες ἕτεραι*, Myonides & Euphranor):
       **7th, 8th, 9th, and 10th** — where the **10th Mean** (:math:`(z - x)/(z - m) = m/x`)
       reduces for :math:`m \ne x` to the Fibonacci additive law :math:`x + m = z`
       (canonical example :math:`3, 5, 8`).

   - **Semicircle Construction of the Three Classical Means** (*cf.* Pappus *Synagoge* III):
     Exact :class:`~geometor.model.Model` construction proving visually :math:`A \ge G \ge H` and
     :math:`A \cdot H = G^2`.
   - **The Most Perfect Proportion (*Τελειοτάτη Ἀναλογία*, II.29)**:
     :math:`6 : 8 :: 9 : 12`, uniting the topology of the **Cube** (``6`` faces, ``8`` vertices,
     ``12`` edges — Philolaus's "geometric harmony"), Arithmetic (``9``), Harmonic (``8``), and
     Geometric (:math:`6 \cdot 12 = 8 \cdot 9 = 72`) symmetry, projective harmonic cross-ratio
     :math:`(6, 12; 8, 0) = -1`, and all Pythagorean musical consonances.

.. py:function:: compute_nicomachean_mean(index: int, x: int | sympy.Expr, z: int | sympy.Expr) -> sympy.Expr

   Compute the exact ``index``-th Nicomachean mean ``m`` between ``0 < x < z`` (II.23–28).

   Note on the **10th Mean**: its defining equation :math:`(z - x)/(z - m) = m/x` factors as
   :math:`(m - x)(m - (z - x)) = 0`; hence a strict interior mean :math:`x < m < z` exists if
   and only if the *diorismos* :math:`z > 2x` holds, in which case :math:`m = z - x`
   (:math:`x + m = z`).

   :param index: Nicomachean proportion ordinal ``1..10``.
   :param x: Lesser positive extreme (concrete number or positive symbolic expression).
   :param z: Greater positive extreme satisfying ``z > x`` (and ``z > 2*x`` when ``index == 10``
             for concrete numerical inputs).

   :returns: Exact simplified :class:`sympy.Expr` for ``m``.

   :raises ValueError: If ``index`` is outside ``1..10`` or concrete bounds violate ``0 < x < z``
       (or ``z <= 2*x`` for ``index == 10``).

.. py:function:: construct_three_classical_means_model(a_len: int | sympy.Expr = 6, c_len: int | sympy.Expr = 12) -> geometor.model.Model

   Build an exact semicircle :class:`~geometor.model.Model` of Arithmetic, Geometric, and Harmonic means.

   Defaults to ``a_len = 6, c_len = 12`` (the Cube extremes of Book II, Ch. 29), where
   ``OS = 9`` (Arithmetic), ``QS = 6*sqrt(2)`` (Geometric), and ``SF = 8`` (Harmonic), all in
   :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})` with ``Q(MeanA) * Q(MeanH) == Q(MeanG)**2 == 5184``.

   :param a_len: First positive segment length ``a``.
   :param c_len: Second positive segment length ``c``.

   :returns: Verified exact symbolic :class:`~geometor.model.Model`.

   :raises ValueError: If concrete inputs are non-positive.


.. py:function:: construct_cube_proportion_model() -> CubeHarmonicResult

   Build and verify Nicomachus's *Teleiotatē Analogia* ``6 : 8 :: 9 : 12`` (Book II, Ch. 29).

   Constructs ``T9(9, 0)`` by Euclidean bisection and ``T8(8, 0)`` by a pure straightedge
   complete quadrilateral from ``Z(0, 0), T6(6, 0), T12(12, 0)``, then verifies:

   - Topological Cube witness: ``6`` faces, ``8`` vertices, ``12`` edges (Euler ``6 + 8 - 12 = 2``).
   - Projective harmonic range ``(T6, T12; T8, Z) == -1``.
   - Musical consonances: octave ``12:6 = 2:1``, fifth ``9:6 = 12:8 = 3:2``, fourth
     ``8:6 = 12:9 = 4:3``, and whole tone ``9:8``.

   :returns: Verified :class:`CubeHarmonicResult`.
