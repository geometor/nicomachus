Mathematical Notes
------------------

* **Coprimality Constraint**: Nicomachus's argument in I.20.2—that :math:`1 + \frac{2}{4} = 1 + \frac{1}{2}` and :math:`1 + \frac{2}{6} = 1 + \frac{1}{3}` collapse into superparticulars—is the classical recognition that the extra parts :math:`r` and denominator :math:`n` must be **coprime** (:math:`\gcd(n, r) = 1`) for the ratio to represent a genuine reduced superpartient.
* **Terminology**: For the fuller compound naming (*epidi-tritos*, *epitri-tetartos*, *epitetra-pemptos*), see :doc:`Chapter 21 <../ch_21/index>`.
