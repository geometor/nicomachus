====================================================================
Chapter 20: Third Species of Inequality: The Superpartient — Summary
====================================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0236
   :page: p. 220

Overview
--------

Nicomachus defines the third species of greater inequality, the :g:`superpartient <five-species-ratios:SP3>` (:term:`epimeres <epimeres>`), together with its correlative, the **subsuperpartient** (*hypepimerēs*):

1. **Definition (I.20.1, 3)**: A number is *superpartient* when it contains the whole of the lesser number plus *more than one* aliquot part of it (:math:`(n + r) : n` with :math:`1 < r < n` and :math:`\gcd(n, r) = 1`)—specifically superbipartient (2 extra parts), supertripartient (3 extra parts), superquadripartient (4 extra parts), etc.
2. **Why the Parts Begin at Thirds and Odd Denominators (I.20.2)**: Two halves added to the whole would yield a double (a multiple, :math:`2:1`), while two fourths equal a half and two sixths equal a third (reducing to superparticulars). Thus the root superbipartient series necessarily begins at :math:`\frac{2}{3}` (:math:`5:3`) and advances through odd denominators :math:`\frac{2}{5}` (:math:`7:5`), :math:`\frac{2}{7}` (:math:`9:7`), :math:`\frac{2}{9}` (:math:`11:9`).
