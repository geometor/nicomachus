Editorial & Mathematical Notes
------------------------------

* **Family of Ratios Generated**: Notice that the pairing :math:`(2k + 3) : (k + 2)` for :math:`k = 1, 2, 3, \dots` yields :math:`\frac{5}{3}, \frac{7}{4}, \frac{9}{5}, \frac{11}{6}, \frac{13}{7}, \dots = 2 - \frac{1}{k+2}`, i.e., the specific subclass of superpartient ratios of the form :math:`(2n - 1) : n` (:math:`n \ge 3`) whose excess parts are :math:`r = n - 1` (necessarily coprime to :math:`n`). Other superpartients such as :math:`7:5` (:math:`1 + \frac{2}{5}`) or :math:`8:5` (:math:`1 + \frac{3}{5}`) appear via the more general Equality Unfolding Tree in :doc:`Chapter 23 <../ch_23/index>`.
* **Table Provenance (Hoche p. 57)**: Hoche notes in his critical apparatus that the *pythmenes* table on p. 57 is supplied from Codex C (*Göttingen*), correcting scribal corruptions in G.
