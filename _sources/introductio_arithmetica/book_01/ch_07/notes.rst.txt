Editorial & Mathematical Notes
------------------------------

* **Classical Definitions of Number (I.7.1)**: The first definition (*plēthos hōrismenon*) derives from :person:`Eudoxus <eudoxus>` (via :person:`Iamblichus <iamblichus>`, :cite:`iamblichus:pistelli:1894`, p. 10), the second (*monadōn systēma*) corresponds to :person:`Euclid <euclid>`, *Elements* VII, Def. 2 (:cite:`euclid:elements:vii-ix`), and the third (*posotētos chyma*) is attributed to Moderatus of Gades.
* **Status of the Dyad (I.7.4)**: Nicomachus excludes the dyad (:math:`2 = 1 + 1`) from unequal two-part partitions because the :term:`monad <monas>` is the indivisible principle (*archē*) of number rather than a proper number itself; cf. :person:`Theon of Smyrna <theon-of-smyrna>` (:cite:`theon:hiller:1878`, p. 22).
