Philosophical & Number-Theoretic Notes
--------------------------------------

* **Aristotelian Mean & Excess/Defect (I.14.2)**: Nicomachus frames arithmetic perfection through :person:`Aristotle's <aristotle>` ethical doctrine of the mean (*mesotēs*) between *hyperbolē* and *elleipsis* (*Nicomachean Ethics* II.6, 1106b33), aligning excess and defect with the two branches of :term:`inequality <isotes>` analyzed in :doc:`Chapter 17 <../ch_17/index>`.
* **Homeric Allusion (I.14.3)**: "Ten tongues ... and ten mouths" echoes Homer, *Iliad* II.489 (also compared by Hoche's apparatus to *Odyssey* XII.89 on Scylla's supernumerary limbs).
* **Terminology (*Hyperteleis* vs. *Hyperteleioi*)**: :person:`Theon of Smyrna <theon-of-smyrna>` (:cite:`theon:hiller:1878`, pp. 45–46) includes the same class under the variant name *hyperteleioi* ("over-perfect"), whereas Nicomachus pairs *hyperteleis* with *ellipeis* ("deficient").
