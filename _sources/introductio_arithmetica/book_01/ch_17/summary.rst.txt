==================================================================================
Chapter 17: Relative Quantity, Equality & the Five Species of Inequality — Summary
==================================================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0228
   :page: p. 212

Overview
--------

Passing from absolute quantity (*to kath' hauto poson*) to relative quantity (*to pros ti poson*), Nicomachus establishes the exhaustive tenfold taxonomy of numerical ratios:

1. **Equality (*Isotēs*, I.17.2–5)**: :g:`Equality (1 : 1) <five-species-ratios:EQ11>` is indivisible, admits no subspecies, and is symmetric (the correlative of "equal" is "equal," like *friend* or *neighbor*).
2. **Inequality (*Anisotēs*, I.17.6–8)**: Inequality splits asymmetrically into **greater** (*meizon*) and **less** (*elatton*), each unfolding into five paired species:
   - :g:`Multiple <five-species-ratios:SP1>` (*pollaplasios*) / Submultiple (*hypopollaplasios*)
   - :g:`Superparticular <five-species-ratios:SP2>` (*epimorios*) / Subsuperparticular (*hypepimorios*)
   - :g:`Superpartient <five-species-ratios:SP3>` (*epimerēs*) / Subsuperpartient (*hypepimerēs*)
   - :g:`Multiple superparticular <five-species-ratios:SP4>` (*pollaplasiepimorios*) / Submultiple-superparticular
   - :g:`Multiple superpartient <five-species-ratios:SP5>` (*pollaplasiepimerēs*) / Submultiple-superpartient
