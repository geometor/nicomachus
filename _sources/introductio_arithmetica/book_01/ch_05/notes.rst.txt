Archival Witnesses & Apparatus
------------------------------

* **English Witness**: D'Ooge (1926), pp. 188–189 (folios ``introductio_arithmetica:0204``–``0205``).
* **Greek Witness**: Hoche (1866), pp. 10–12 (folios ``hoche_1866:0028``–``0030``).
* **Boethius Parallel**: :person:`Boethius <boethius>`, *De Institutione Arithmetica* I.1 (:cite:`boethius:friedlein:1867`, pp. 11–12 Friedlein).

Philosophical & Musical Notes
-----------------------------

* **Notice on Hoche vs. D'Ooge (I.5.1)**: Hoche's Greek text explicitly lists the fifth consonance *triplasios de hē dia pasōn hama kai dia pente* (:math:`3:1`, octave plus fifth) between the *diapason* (:math:`2:1`) and the *dis-diapason* (:math:`4:1`). D'Ooge omits that clause in his translation (following the variant tradition noted in his apparatus).
* **Plato on Motion after Rest (I.5.2)**: The hierarchy placing solid geometry before astronomy reflects Plato's *Republic* VII (528a–b), where Socrates warns against studying bodies in motion before stereometry *per se*.
