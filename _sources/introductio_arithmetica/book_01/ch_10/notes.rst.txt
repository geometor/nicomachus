Chapter 10: The Odd-Times Even Number — Notes
=============================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0214

Archival Witnesses & Apparatus
------------------------------

* **English Witness**: D'Ooge (1926), pp. 198–201 (folios ``introductio_arithmetica:0214``–``0217``).
* **Greek Witness**: Hoche (1866), pp. 21–25 (folios ``hoche_1866:0039``–``0043``).
* **Boethius Parallel**: :person:`Boethius <boethius>`, *De Institutione Arithmetica* I.11–12 (:cite:`boethius:friedlein:1867`, pp. 24–28 Friedlein).

Editorial & Mathematical Notes
------------------------------

* **Complete Disjoint Partition of Even Numbers**: Together with Chs. 8 and 9, Ch. 10 partitions every even integer :math:`n \ge 2` uniquely into :math:`2^k` (even-times even, :math:`k \ge 1`), :math:`2(2m+1)` (even-times odd, :math:`m \ge 1`), or :math:`2^k(2m+1)` (odd-times even, :math:`k \ge 2, m \ge 1`). Contrast :person:`Euclid <euclid>` (*Elements* VII, Defs. 8–10, :cite:`euclid:elements:vii-ix`), where *even-times even* and *even-times odd* overlap; :person:`Philoponus <philoponus>` (:cite:`philoponus:hoche:1864`, on I.10) notes the superiority of the Nicomachean tripartition.
* **Hoche Table Reading (p. 25, row 5, col. 6)**: In the multiplication table on Hoche p. 25 (``hoche_1866:0043``), the entry for :math:`11 \times 128 = 1408` appears printed as ``͵ανη`` (a typographical confusion of *upsilon* for *nu*, *recte* ``͵αυη``); the English track prints the exact product :math:`1408`.
