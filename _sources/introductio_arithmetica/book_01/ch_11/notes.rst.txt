Editorial & Mathematical Notes
------------------------------

* **Nicomachus vs. Euclid on Primality**: Unlike Euclid (:cite:`euclid:heath:1926`, *Elements* VII, Def. 11), who defines a prime over all numbers (including :math:`2`), Nicomachus treats primality strictly as a subdivision of **odd** numbers, reserving :math:`2` as the archetypal root of the even.
* **Hoche Bracketed Clause (p. 26)**: Following Codex G and manuscript consensus, Hoche brackets the gloss *τὸ ἑαυτοῦ παρώνυμον καὶ τοῦτο πάντως μονάδα* in §2; the present Greek track reflects Hoche's critical text.
