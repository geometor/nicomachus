Philosophical Notes
-------------------

* **Pythagorean Etymology of Philosophy**: Nicomachus transmits the classical tradition (also attested in Cicero *Tusc.* V.3.8 and Diogenes Laertius I.12) that :person:`Pythagoras <pythagoras>` narrowed *sophia* from technical craftsmanship (*techne*) to the *episteme* of eternal immaterial reality.
* **Matter and Incorporeal Accidents (I.1.3)**: In *In Nicomachi Arithmeticam*, John Philoponus notes Ammonius's critique of calling matter *trepte kai alloiote* ("changeable and alterable") rather than *treptike kai alloiotike* ("capable of receiving change"), since Nicomachus blends Middle-Platonic dualism with Aristotelian categories (*quality, quantity, relation, place, time*).
* **Quadrivium Context**: See :doc:`Chapter 3 <../ch_03/index>` for the formal bifurcation of quantity into the four sister sciences (*mathemata*).
