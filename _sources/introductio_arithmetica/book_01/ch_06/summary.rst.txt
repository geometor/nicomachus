=========================================================================
Chapter 6: Number as Cosmic Exemplar in the Mind of the Creator — Summary
=========================================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0205
   :page: p. 189

Overview
--------

Bridging the Quadrivium prologue (Chs. 1–5) to the internal classification of numbers (Chs. 7–16), Nicomachus grounds cosmic order in the self-harmonized polarity of number:

1. **Pre-existent Divine Paradigm (I.6.1)**: Purely intelligible, immaterial, and eternal :term:`number <arithmos>` pre-exists in the mind of the Demiurge as the *procharagma* (preliminary sketch) and *logos technikos* governing time, celestial motion, stars, and cosmic cycles.
2. **Transcendental Conditions of Harmony (I.6.2–3)**: Because scientific number imparts harmony to the cosmos, it must first be harmonized within itself. Every harmony requires constituent elements that (a) actually exist, (b) differ from one another, and (c) stand in rational proportion (*logos*).
3. **Even and Odd as Primary Species (I.6.4)**: These conditions are fulfilled by the two primary, complementary species of quantity—**even** (*artion*) and **odd** (*peritton*)—inseparably interwoven by divine nature.
