Philosophical Notes
-------------------

* **Poson vs. Pēlikon and Aristotle's Categories**: In :cite:`aristotle:cat:4b20` (*Categories* 6, 4b20), :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` divided quantity (*to poson*) into discrete and continuous. Nicomachus sharpens this nomenclature by reserving :term:`poson <poson>` (*multitudo*) strictly for bounded discrete multitude and :term:`pelikon <pelikon>` (*magnitudo*) for bounded continuous magnitude.
* **Proclus and Hero**: :person:`Proclus <proclus>` (*In Prim. Eucl. Elem.*, pp. 6.15 and 36.3 Friedlein) preserves the exact Pythagorean distinction between the discrete infinite by addition and the continuous infinite by division.
