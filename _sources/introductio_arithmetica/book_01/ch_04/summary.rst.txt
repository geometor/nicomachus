======================================================================
Chapter 4: Ontological Priority of Arithmetic via Co-Removal — Summary
======================================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0203
   :page: p. 187

Overview
--------

Nicomachus establishes :g:`arithmetic <quadrivium-tetrad:ARITH>` as the foundational "mother" (*mētēr*) of the mathematical sciences on two grounds:

1. **Cosmological Priority (I.4.2)**: Arithmetic pre-exists in the mind of the Demiurge as a cosmic and archetypal paradigm (*prokentēma*, *archetypon paradeigma*) according to which material creation is ordered.
2. **Logical Priority by Asymmetric Co-Removal (I.4.2–5)**: Adapting the Academic–Aristotelian test (:term:`synanairein`), $A$ is naturally prior to $B$ if removing $A$ removes $B$, whereas removing $B$ leaves $A$ intact (as *animal* precedes *man*, or *man* precedes *schoolteacher*). Because :g:`geometry <quadrivium-tetrad:GEOM>` presupposes numerical concepts (*triangle* = 3, *quadrilateral* = 4, *octahedron* = 8, *icosahedron* = 20, *triple*, *eightfold*) while numbers exist independently of spatial figures, arithmetic co-removes geometry without being co-removed by it.
