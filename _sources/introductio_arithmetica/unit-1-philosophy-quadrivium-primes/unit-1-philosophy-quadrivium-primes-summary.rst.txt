Architectural Thesis
--------------------

Unit I establishes the philosophical charter of the Western mathematical curriculum before classifying odd primes and even perfect numbers:

1. **Immaterial Reality & the Dichotomy of Quantity (I.1–2)**: Following :person:`Pythagoras <pythagoras>` and :person:`Plato's <plato>` *Timaeus* (:cite:`plato:timaeus:27d-36d`), true wisdom studies eternal immaterial realities rather than bodily flux. :g:`Quantity <quadrivium-tetrad:QUANT>` bifurcates into discontinuous :g:`Multitude <quadrivium-tetrad:MULT>` (:term:`poson <poson>`, bounded below by the :term:`monad <monas>` and increasing without limit) and continuous :g:`Magnitude <quadrivium-tetrad:MAGN>` (:term:`pelikon <pelikon>`, bounded as a whole and infinitely divisible).
2. **The Four Sister Sciences & Asymmetric Priority (I.3–6)**: Quoting :person:`Archytas <archytas>` (:cite:`archytas:dk47b1`), Nicomachus maps absolute vs. relative multitude to :g:`Arithmetic <quadrivium-tetrad:ARITH>` and :g:`Music <quadrivium-tetrad:MUSIC>`, and stationary vs. revolving magnitude to :g:`Geometry <quadrivium-tetrad:GEOM>` and :g:`Astronomy <quadrivium-tetrad:ASTRON>`. By :term:`asymmetric co-removal <synanairein>`, :g:`Arithmetic precedes all three sister sciences <quadrivium-tetrad:PRIORITY>` and exists pre-eminently as the cosmic exemplar in the Mind of the Creator (I.6.1).
3. **The Odd Sieve of Eratosthenes & Perfect Numbers (I.7, 13, 16)**: Operating strictly on odd numbers :math:`\ge 3`, :person:`Eratosthenes's <eratosthenes>` :term:`Sieve <koskinon>` steps by :math:`p` positions along the odd sequence (:math:`2p` in integers) to separate primes from odd composites, while :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` formula :math:`2^{p-1}(2^p - 1)` generates the :term:`perfect numbers <teleios>` :math:`6, 28, 496, 8128`.
