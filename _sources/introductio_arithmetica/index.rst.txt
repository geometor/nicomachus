===========================================================================================
Introductio Arithmetica (Ἀριθμητικὴ Εἰσαγωγή) — Quadrivium, Ratios, Gnomons & Cube Harmony
===========================================================================================

.. rubric:: Synchronized 4-Track Book Codex (English · Greek Hoche 1866 · Summary · Critical Notes)

**Introductio Arithmetica** (*Ἀριθμητικὴ Εἰσαγωγή*, Books I–II) of :person:`Nicomachus of Gerasa <nicomachus>` (c. 60–120 CE) is the foundational Neo-Pythagorean treatise that organized the **Four Mathematical Sciences** (:term:`mathemata <mathemata>`, rendered *quadruvium* by :person:`Boethius <boethius>`), unfolded all rational ratios from primordial :term:`Equality <isotes>` (:math:`1 : 1 : 1`), constructed polygonal and solid numbers by arithmetic :term:`gnomons <gnomon>`, proved the **Sum-of-Cubes Theorem** (:math:`\sum_{k=1}^n k^3 = T_n^2`), and crowned :math:`6 : 8 :: 9 : 12` as the **Most Perfect Three-Dimensional Proportion** (:term:`teleiotate analogia <teleiotate-analogia>`) uniting the Cube and the Pythagorean musical scale.

Every unit is presented across **four synchronized tracks**—**English** (:person:`Martin Luther D'Ooge's <dooge>` public-domain translation, 1926; :cite:`dooge:1926`), **Greek (Hoche 1866)** (:person:`Richard Hoche's <hoche>` Teubner critical edition, 1866; :cite:`hoche:1866`), **Summary**, and **Critical Notes** (pairing interactive ``.euclid-graphic`` SVG diagrams and exact ``SymPy`` / Rational Trigonometry proofs with :person:`Boethius's <boethius>` *De Institutione Arithmetica* [:cite:`boethius:friedlein:1867`] and the GEOMETOR canon).
