Chapter 2: Inverse Three-Subtraction Reduction to Equality (1, 1, 1) — Summary
==============================================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0246
   :page: p. 230

Overview
--------

Nicomachus states the universal :g:`inverse three-subtraction algorithm <equality-unfolding-tree:REDUCE>` that reduces any continuous three-term geometric proportion back to :g:`fundamental equality (1, 1, 1) <equality-unfolding-tree:ROOT111>`:

1. **The Three Subtractions (II.2.1–2)**: Given any three terms :math:`(x, y, z)` in continuous ratio (arranged with the least extreme first if necessary), set :math:`x' = x`, :math:`y' = y - x`, and :math:`z' = z - x - 2(y - x)`.
2. **Descent to Equality (II.2.3)**: Iterating these three subtractions—reversing the order whenever the third term becomes smallest—steps through progressively more fundamental ratios until all three terms become equal, proving that :term:`equality <isotes>` is the irreducible element of all relative numbers.
3. **Harmonic Application (II.2.4)**: Nicomachus notes that the reverse generation rules provide an exact method for constructing chains of superparticular intervals (fifths :math:`3:2`, fourths :math:`4:3`, whole tones :math:`9:8`) needed in :person:`Plato's <plato>` *Timaeus* psychogony and harmonic theory.
