==========================================================
Chapter 9: Square Numbers and Odd-Number Gnomons — Summary
==========================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0258
   :page: p. 242

Overview
--------

Nicomachus defines and constructs the :g:`square numbers <polygonal-alpha-gnomons:SQR>` via odd-number :term:`gnomons <gnomon>`:

1. **Equilateral Four-Angled Figures (II.9.1–2)**: Square numbers (:math:`1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100, \dots`) form four-angled equilateral arrays whose sides advance as :math:`1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, \dots`, with :math:`1` as the potential square and :math:`4` as the first actual square.
2. **Generation from Odd Numbers (II.9.3)**: Leaving out one term at a time from the natural series—summing the consecutive odd numbers :math:`1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 + 11 + \dots`—generates every square :math:`n^2`, with the side of each square equal to the number of odd gnomons summed.
