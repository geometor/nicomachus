========================================================
Chapter 9: Square Numbers and Odd-Number Gnomons — Notes
========================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0258
   :page: p. 242

Archival Witnesses & Apparatus
------------------------------

* **English Witness**: D'Ooge (1926), pp. 242–243 (folios ``introductio_arithmetica:0258``–``0259``).
* **Greek Witness**: Hoche (1866), pp. 90–91 (folios ``hoche_1866:0108``–``0109``).
* **Boethius Parallel**: :person:`Boethius <boethius>`, *De Institutione Arithmetica* II.7 (:cite:`boethius:friedlein:1867`, pp. 94–96 Friedlein).

Mathematical & Textual Notes
----------------------------

* **Foundation for Book II, Ch. 20**: The odd-summation identity :math:`\sum_{m=1}^{n} (2m - 1) = n^2` established here is the exact lemma underlying Nicomachus's Sum-of-Cubes Theorem in :doc:`Chapter 20 <../ch_20/index>` (:math:`\sum_{k=1}^n k^3 = T_n^2`).
* **Manuscript Schema (Hoche p. 91)**: Hoche notes that Codices *G* and *Γ* add a three-row table contrasting the skipped even numbers (:math:`2, 4, 6, 8, \dots`), the odd gnomons (:math:`1, 3, 5, 7, \dots`), and the resulting squares (:math:`1, 4, 9, 16, \dots`).
