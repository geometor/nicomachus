Mathematical & Historical Notes
-------------------------------

* **Euler–Philolaus Cube Duality & Projective Conjugate**: In ``geometor.nicomachus.construct_cube_proportion_model``, :math:`T_9 = A(6, 12)` is constructed by Euclidean bisection and :g:`T_8 = H(6, 12) <cube-most-perfect-proportion:VERTS8>` by complete-quadrilateral harmonic conjugation with exact cross-ratio $(T_6, T_{12}; T_8, Z) = -1$.
* **Universal Mean Identity**: $A \cdot H = G^2 \implies 9 \times 8 = 6 \times 12 = 72$ (verified in ``verify_cube_proportion_identities``), generating disjunct geometric proportions $6 : 8 :: 9 : 12$ and $6 : 9 :: 8 : 12$.
* **Musical Scaffolding & Reception**: Dividing a :g:`monochord <cube-most-perfect-proportion:MONOCHORD>` at $6, 8, 9, 12$ sounds the :g:`fourth <cube-most-perfect-proportion:FOURTH>`, :g:`fifth <cube-most-perfect-proportion:FIFTH>`, :g:`octave <cube-most-perfect-proportion:OCTAVE>`, and :g:`whole tone <cube-most-perfect-proportion:TONE>`, echoing :person:`Plato's <plato>` *Timaeus* 35b–36b and influencing :person:`Boethius <boethius>`, :person:`Luca Pacioli <pacioli>`, and :person:`Johannes Kepler <kepler>`.
