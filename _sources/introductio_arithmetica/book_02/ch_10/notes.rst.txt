Chapter 10: Pentagonal Numbers and Gnomons Differing by Three — Notes
=====================================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0259
   :page: p. 243

Archival Witnesses & Apparatus
------------------------------

* **English Witness**: D'Ooge (1926), pp. 243–244 (folios ``introductio_arithmetica:0259``–``0260``).
* **Greek Witness**: Hoche (1866), pp. 92–93 (folios ``hoche_1866:0110``–``0111``).
* **Boethius Parallel**: :person:`Boethius <boethius>`, *De Institutione Arithmetica* II.8 (:cite:`boethius:friedlein:1867`, pp. 96–98 Friedlein).

Mathematical & Textual Notes
----------------------------

* **Alpha-Pebble Geometry**: In the manuscript diagrams (`hoche_1866:0111`), each pentagonal array is drawn as a square topped by an isosceles roof—illustrating geometrically why :math:`P(5, n) = S_n + T_{n-1}`, as proved explicitly in :doc:`Chapter 12 <../ch_12/index>`.
* **Marginal Generation Diagram (Hoche p. 93)**: Codex *G* (*Γ*) includes a vertical interlocking-arc diagram on p. 93 showing the natural integers :math:`1 \dots 24`, the selected gnomons :math:`1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22`, and the resulting pentagonals up to :math:`330`.
