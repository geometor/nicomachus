=======================================================================
Chapter 10: Pentagonal Numbers and Gnomons Differing by Three — Summary
=======================================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0259
   :page: p. 243

Overview
--------

Nicomachus defines and constructs the :g:`pentagonal numbers <polygonal-alpha-gnomons:PENT>`, preparing the general polygonal rule of Chapter 11:

1. **Five-Angled Arrays (II.10.1–2)**: Pentagonal numbers (:math:`1, 5, 12, 22, 35, 51, 70, 92, 117, \dots`) are five-angled equilateral plane figures whose sides advance by unity (:math:`1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, \dots`) according to the number of summed terms.
2. **Gnomons Differing by Three (II.10.2–3)**: Skipping two consecutive integers at each step in the natural series selects the sequence :math:`1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25, \dots` (common difference :math:`3`); accumulating these :term:`gnomons <gnomon>` produces :math:`1`, :math:`1+4=5`, :math:`5+7=12`, :math:`12+10=22`, :math:`22+13=35`, and so on via :math:`P(5, n) = \frac{n(3n - 1)}{2}`.
