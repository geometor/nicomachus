=============================================================
Chapter 10: Pentagonal Numbers and Gnomons Differing by Three
=============================================================

.. picture:: /_static/figures/polygonal-alpha-gnomons.svg
   :alt: Polygonal Numbers Built by Alpha-Pebble Gnomons and Decomposed into Triangles
   :caption: Book II, Chs. 8–10: Alpha-pebble (α) layouts of Triangular (d=1), Square (d=2), and Pentagonal (d=3) numbers built by successive arithmetic gnomons.

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0259
   :page: p. 243

**1.** A :g:`pentagonal number <polygonal-alpha-gnomons:PENT>` is one which, likewise when expanded into units, takes plane shape as a pentagonal figure equal on all sides—such as :math:`1, 5, 12, 22, 35, 51, 70`, and those in the same proportion.
**2.** Now of the first actual pentagon, :math:`5`, each side is :math:`2`—for :math:`1` is the side of the very first potential pentagon, unity; of the second set forth, :math:`12`, the side is :math:`3`; of the next, :math:`22`, it is :math:`4`; of the following, :math:`35`, it is :math:`5`; of :math:`51` after it, :math:`6`; and so onward. Universally, the side has as many units as the numbers chosen from the natural row and heaped together to form it. For similarly, numbers from unity skipping two at a time—those exceeding one another by :math:`3`—are piled together to generate the pentagon:

unity is first potentially; :math:`5` is second, made from :math:`1 + 4`; and :math:`12` is third, from the first two together with :math:`7` piled onto them so that it may have a side of :math:`3`, just as :math:`5` before it had a side of :math:`2`:

.. code-block:: text

   [1]     [2]       [3]
    α       α         α
           α α       α α
           α α     α  α  α
                   α  α  α
                   α  α  α

**3.** Those that follow will arise by successively adding the terms advancing by an excess of :math:`3` after :math:`7`, namely :math:`10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25`, to infinity; the resulting pentagons will be :math:`22, 35, 51, 70, 92, 117`, and this forever.
