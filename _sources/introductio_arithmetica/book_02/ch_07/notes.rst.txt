================================================================================
Chapter 7: Point, Line, Surface, and the Triangle as Element of Polygons — Notes
================================================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0255
   :page: p. 239

Archival Witnesses & Apparatus
------------------------------

* **English Witness**: D'Ooge (1926), pp. 239–241 (folios ``introductio_arithmetica:0255``–``0257``).
* **Greek Witness**: Hoche (1866), pp. 86–87 (folios ``hoche_1866:0104``–``0105``).
* **Boethius Parallel**: :person:`Boethius <boethius>`, *De Institutione Arithmetica* II.5–6 (:cite:`boethius:friedlein:1867`, pp. 90–92 Friedlein).

Mathematical & Textual Notes
----------------------------

* **Geometrical Triangulation**: D'Ooge (p. 240 n. 4) notes that Hoche's manuscripts (*G*, *P*, *S*) insert a marginal figure showing polygons divided from an interior center into triangles—illustrating II.7.4's claim that every :math:`s`-gon divides into :math:`s` triangles.
* **Foreshadowing Chapter 12**: The arithmetic confirmation promised at the end of II.7.5 is carried out systematically in :doc:`Chapter 12 <../ch_12/index>`, where every :math:`s`-gonal number decomposes into :math:`T_n + (s - 3)T_{n-1}`.
