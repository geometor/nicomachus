Chapter 16: Scalene Solids, Wedges, Altars & Parallelepipeds — Summary
======================================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0270
   :page: p. 254

Overview
--------

Nicomachus contrasts the totally equilateral cube with the totally unequal scalene solid and locates parallelepipeds as their intermediate mean:

1. **Scalene Solids (II.16.1)**: Solids with three mutually unequal factors $a \times b \times c$ (e.g. $2 \times 3 \times 4 = 24$, $2 \times 4 \times 8 = 64$, $3 \times 5 \times 12 = 180$).
2. **Traditional Epithets (II.16.2)**: Surveys craft and architectural nicknames for scalenes—*wedges* (*sphēniskoi*), *wasp-bodies* (*sphēkiskoi*), and *altars* (*bōmiskoi*).
3. **Parallelepipeds as Mean (II.16.3)**: Intermediate between cubes (equal faces) and scalenes (all unequal), parallelepipeds have :term:`heteromecic <heteromekes>` faces $n(n+1)$ rather than squares.
