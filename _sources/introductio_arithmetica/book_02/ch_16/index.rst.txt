============================================================
Chapter 16: Scalene Solids, Wedges, Altars & Parallelepipeds
============================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0270
   :page: p. 254

**1.** Since the cube is a solid figure equal-sided throughout—in length, depth, and breadth—and equally dimensioned across the so-called six orientations, it follows directly that opposed to it stands the solid whose dimensions are nowhere equal to one another, having depth unequal to breadth, and length unequal to both of them, such as twice three times four, or twice four times eight, or thrice five times twelve, or arranged by some other inequality of this sort.

**2.** Solid figures of this kind, all of whose dimensions differ from one another, are termed simply *scalenes*. Some designate them by various names and call them *wedges* (*sphēniskoi*), for wedges fashioned by carpenters, builders, metalworkers, and practitioners of other crafts are unequal-sided in every direction, entering from a sharper edge and broadening unequally along every dimension.

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0271
   :page: p. 255

Others call these same numbers *wasp-bodies* (*sphēkiskoi*), since the body of wasps in particular is pinched inward at the middle and displays the likeness just mentioned; from this, likely enough, the term *sphēkōma* (cinching) also took its name, for wherever a thing is drawn tight, it mimics the wasp's waist. Still others name them *altars* (*bōmiskoi*) from an apt comparison; for archaic altars, and especially Ionic ones, neither have breadth equal to depth, nor these two equal to length, nor base equal to summit, but vary in all their dimensions.

**3.** Accordingly, midway between two extremes—the cube and the scalene, the one extended in complete equality and the other in complete inequality—stand the solid numbers called *parallelepipeds*, whose plane faces are heteromecic numbers, just as the faces of cubes themselves were square numbers, as has already been shown.
