Mathematical & Historical Notes
-------------------------------

* **Superparticular Sequence from Oblongs & Squares**: Alternating squares and oblongs (:math:`1, 2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 16, 20, \dots`) in overlapping triads—:math:`(1, 2, 4)`, :math:`(4, 6, 9)`, :math:`(9, 12, 16)`, :math:`(16, 20, 25)`—yields successive geometric progressions of ratios :math:`2:1, 3:2, 4:3, 5:4, \dots`.
* **Platonic Dimensional Bonding**: Two squares :math:`p^2, q^2` have one mean :math:`pq` (two intervals), explaining why planes are bonded by one mean in *Timaeus* 32b; two cubes :math:`p^3, q^3` require two means :math:`p^2q, pq^2` (three intervals), explaining solid bonding.
* **Geometric Realization**: Compare :g:`ordinate QS = G <three-classical-means-semicircle:QS>` on :g:`semicircle PR <three-classical-means-semicircle:SEMICIRC>` in ``geometor.nicomachus.construct_three_classical_means_model``.
