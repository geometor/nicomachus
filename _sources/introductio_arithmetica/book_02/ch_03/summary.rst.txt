Chapter 3: Multiples Generating Corresponding Superparticular Ratios — Summary
==============================================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0248
   :page: p. 232

Overview
--------

Nicomachus answers the musical problem posed at the end of Chapter 2 by proving how powers of a multiple generate exact finite chains of corresponding :term:`superparticular <epimorios>` ratios:

1. **The Rank-Count Law (II.3.1–2)**: The :math:`k`-th multiple from unity (:math:`m^k`) heads a chain of **exactly** :math:`k` consecutive superparticular ratios of the corresponding denominator (:math:`(m+1)/m`), terminating naturally at :math:`(m+1)^k`, which is indivisible by :math:`m`.
2. **Doubles and Sesquialters (II.3.3–4)**: In the geometric progression of doubles :math:`1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64`, :math:`2` yields one :term:`sesquialter <hemiolios>` (:math:`3`), :math:`4` yields two (:math:`6, 9`), :math:`8` yields three (:math:`12, 18, 27`), and :math:`16` yields four (:math:`24, 36, 54, 81`), arranged in the triangular *Diplasiōn diagramma*. Along its hypotenuse run the triple powers :math:`1, 3, 9, 27, 81, 243, 729`.
