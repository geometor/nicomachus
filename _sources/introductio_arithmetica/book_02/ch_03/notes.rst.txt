Archival Witnesses & Apparatus
------------------------------

* **Boethius Parallel**: :person:`Boethius <boethius>`, *De Institutione Arithmetica* II.2 (:cite:`boethius:friedlein:1867`).

Mathematical & Textual Notes
----------------------------

* **Algebraic Structure**: The :math:`k`-th column of the double diagram consists of :math:`2^{k-j} \cdot 3^j` for :math:`j = 0, \dots, k`; each vertical step multiplies by :math:`3/2` until :math:`3^k` (odd, no integer half) halts the chain.
* **Manuscript Apparatus (Hoche p. 77)**: Codices *C* and *S* extend the double diagram to an eighth column beginning with :math:`128` (``ρκη``) and ending with :math:`2187` (``͵βρπζ``).
