Thomas Aquinas
==============

- **Greek / Latin**: *Θωμᾶς Ἀκινάτης*  |  **Role**: Scholastic philosopher & theologian
- **Nicomachus Loci**: ``Reception via Boethius I.1–2``

Received Nicomachean discrete-vs.-continuous quantity, relative ratio, and the hierarchy of the Quadrivium through Boethius in his *Commentary on Boethius's De Trinitate* (``geometor/aquinas``).
