=====================
GEOMETOR • nicomachus
=====================

.. rubric:: *Introductio Arithmetica* (*Ἀριθμητικὴ Εἰσαγωγή*) — Nicomachus of Gerasa on the Quadrivium, Ratio Unfolding, Figurate Gnomons & Harmonic Proportion

Critical four-track Digital Scriptorium Book Codex and exact symbolic geometry & number-theory suite for :person:`Nicomachus of Gerasa's <nicomachus>` *Introduction to Arithmetic* (c. 100 CE)—the foundational Neo-Pythagorean treatise that organized the **Four Mathematical Sciences** (:term:`mathemata <mathemata>`, rendered *quadruvium* by :person:`Boethius <boethius>`), unfolded all rational inequalities from primordial :term:`Equality <isotes>` (:math:`1 : 1 : 1`), constructed polygonal and solid numbers by arithmetic :term:`gnomons <gnomon>`, proved the **Sum-of-Cubes Theorem**, and crowned :math:`6 : 8 :: 9 : 12` as the **Most Perfect Three-Dimensional Proportion** uniting the Cube and the Pythagorean musical scale.

Every Codex unit is presented across **four synchronized tracks** powered by ``photon-platform/publish``:

1. **English** — Public-domain critical translation (1926) of :person:`Martin Luther D'Ooge <dooge>` (:cite:`dooge:1926`), segmented to exact chapter and page folio markers.
2. **Greek (Hoche 1866)** — Verbatim polytonic Greek from :person:`Richard Hoche's <hoche>` Teubner critical edition (:cite:`hoche:1866`).
3. **Summary** — Deductive architecture, structural tables, and interactive diagram walkthroughs.
4. **Critical Notes** — Exact ``SymPy`` / Rational Trigonometry bridges, interactive ``.euclid-graphic`` SVGs (``:g:`` / ``:eq:``), :person:`Boethius <boethius>` *De Institutione Arithmetica* (:cite:`boethius:friedlein:1867`) parallels, and philological corrections (including :person:`Frank Egleston Robbins's <robbins>` notes on Means 8–9 in II.28).
