Chapter 29: The Most Perfect Proportion (6 : 8 :: 9 : 12) — Notes¶

Archival Witnesses & Apparatus¶

  • English Witness: Martin Luther D’Ooge (trans.), Nicomachus of Gerasa: Introduction to Arithmetic (University of Michigan Studies, Vol. XVI, 1926), pp. 284–286 (folios introductio_arithmetica:0300–0302).

  • Greek Witness: Richard Hoche (ed.), Nicomachi Geraseni Pythagorei Introductionis Arithmeticae Libri II (Leipzig: Teubner, 1866), pp. 144–147 (folios hoche_1866:0162–0165).

  • Ancient Parallels: Plato, Timaeus 35b–36b; Iamblichus, In Nicomachi Arithmeticam pp. 168–172 Pistelli; Boethius, De Institutione Arithmetica II.54 (maxima et perfecta symphonia).

Mathematical & Historical Notes¶
