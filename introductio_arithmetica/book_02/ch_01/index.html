Chapter 1: Element as Minimum & Equality as Principle of Relative Number¶

1. That is called and truly is an element (stoicheion) which is the smallest thing from which anything is put together and the least into which it is resolved. Thus letters are called the elements of written speech, since from them all articulate utterance is constructed and into them at last it is resolved; musical notes are the elements of every melody, from which it begins to be composed and into which it is resolved; and commonly the so-called four elements of the cosmos are simple bodies—fire, water, air, and earth—for natural philosophy holds that the universe is compounded out of these first and is conceived as resolving back into them at last.

2. In like manner, we wish to prove that equality is the element of relative quantity; for in absolute quantity considered by itself, the monad and the dyad were the primary elements, from which as least constituents it is endlessly compounded and increased and in which its downward resolution comes to rest. We have already shown that the forward progression of inequality into all relations whatsoever arises in due order from equality by three rules. It remains, in order that equality may truly be an element, to prove that all resolutions also terminate in it at last; and for this one must know the following universal method.
