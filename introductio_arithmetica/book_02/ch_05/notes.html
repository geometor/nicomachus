Chapter 5: Composition and Resolution of Multiples from Superparticulars — Notes¶

Archival Witnesses & Apparatus¶

  • English Witness: D’Ooge (1926), pp. 234–236 (folios introductio_arithmetica:0250–0252).

  • Greek Witness: Hoche (1866), pp. 80–82 (folios hoche_1866:0098–0100).

  • Boethius Parallel: Boethius, De Institutione Arithmetica II.3 (boethius:friedlein:1867).

Mathematical & Textual Notes¶

  • Musical Harmonics: In Pythagorean music theory, II.5.1 expresses the foundational octave identity \(\frac{3}{2} \times \frac{4}{3} = 2\) (fifth plus fourth equals octave).

  • Manuscript Scholia (Hoche p. 82): Hoche’s apparatus preserves the Byzantine arc diagram (schemata) illustrating \(\beta, \gamma, \delta\) with upper arcs marked hemiolios (\(2:3\)) and epitritos (\(3:4\)) spanned beneath by diplasios (\(2:4\)).
