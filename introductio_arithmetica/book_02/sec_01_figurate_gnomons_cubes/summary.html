Unit III — Inverse Reduction, Polygonal Gnomons, Same/Other & Sum of Cubes — Structural Summary¶

Figurate Arithmetical Geometry & Gnomonic Identities · Book II, Chs. 1–2, 6–20

Architectural Thesis¶

Unit III completes Nicomachus’s philosophy of Equality by proving the inverse reduction theorem (II.1–2) and then constructs the entire hierarchy of plane and solid figurate numbers from alpha-pebble gnomons (II.6–20):

  1. Inverse Reduction to Equality (II.1–2): Subtracting \((x, y, z) \mapsto (x, y-x, z-x-2(y-x))\) and sorting if needed reduces every geometric progression uniquely back to (1, 1, 1).

  2. Universal Polygonal Gnomon & Triangle Decomposition Laws (II.6–12): Summing arithmetic progressions with difference \(d = s - 2\) builds all \(s\)-gonal numbers \(P(s, n) = n + (s-2)\frac{n(n-1)}{2}\), each decomposing into elementary triangles via P(s, n) = Tn + (s - 3)Tn-1.

  3. Plato’s Same & Other and Nicomachus’s Cube Theorem (II.16–20): Odd gnomons build Squares n2 (Same), even gnomons build Heteromecics n(n+1) (Other), and consecutive odd blocks of length k sum to k3, proving \(\sum_{k=1}^n k^3 = T_n^2\).
