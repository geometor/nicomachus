Unit II — Five Species of Inequality & the Equality Unfolding Tree — Structural Map¶

Ratio Taxonomy & Ternary Tree Generation · Book I, Chs. 17–23

Architectural Thesis¶

Unit II transitions from absolute to relative quantity (pros ti poson), proving that every rational inequality is classified into five exact species and generated deterministically from Equality:

  1. Exhaustive Fivefold Classification of Ratios (I.17–22): Every reduced rational ratio \(a : b\) (\(a > b\), \(\gcd(a, b) = 1\)) written via Euclidean division \(a = m \cdot b + r\) (\(m \ge 1, 0 \le r < b\)) falls into one and only one of five species depending on whether \(m = 1\) vs. \(m \ge 2\) and \(r = 0\), \(r = 1\), or \(r \ge 2\).

  2. Nicomachus’s Rule of Three Terms (I.23): Applying the linear operator \(L(a, b, c) = (a, a+b, a+2b+c)\) directly (\(D = L\)) and after reversal (\(R = L \circ \text{rev}\)) to Equality (1, 1, 1) unfolds a complete binary tree of three-term geometric progressions \((p^2, pq, q^2)\) isomorphic to the Calkin–Wilf / Stern–Brocot tree of positive rationals!
