Chapter 17: Relative Quantity, Equality & the Five Species of Inequality — Summary¶

Overview¶

Passing from absolute quantity (to kath’ hauto poson) to relative quantity (to pros ti poson), Nicomachus establishes the exhaustive tenfold taxonomy of numerical ratios:

  1. Equality (*Isotēs*, I.17.2–5): Equality (1 : 1) is indivisible, admits no subspecies, and is symmetric (the correlative of “equal” is “equal,” like friend or neighbor).

  2. Inequality (*Anisotēs*, I.17.6–8): Inequality splits asymmetrically into greater (meizon) and less (elatton), each unfolding into five paired species: - Multiple (pollaplasios) / Submultiple (hypopollaplasios) - Superparticular (epimorios) / Subsuperparticular (hypepimorios) - Superpartient (epimerēs) / Subsuperpartient (hypepimerēs) - Multiple superparticular (pollaplasiepimorios) / Submultiple-superparticular - Multiple superpartient (pollaplasiepimerēs) / Submultiple-superpartient
