Archival Witnesses & Apparatus
------------------------------

* **English Witness**: D'Ooge (1926), pp. 234–236 (folios ``introductio_arithmetica:0250``–``0252``).
* **Greek Witness**: Hoche (1866), pp. 80–82 (folios ``hoche_1866:0098``–``0100``).
* **Boethius Parallel**: :person:`Boethius <boethius>`, *De Institutione Arithmetica* II.3 (:cite:`boethius:friedlein:1867`).

Mathematical & Textual Notes
----------------------------

* **Musical Harmonics**: In Pythagorean music theory, II.5.1 expresses the foundational octave identity :math:`\frac{3}{2} \times \frac{4}{3} = 2` (fifth plus fourth equals octave).
* **Manuscript Scholia (Hoche p. 82)**: Hoche's apparatus preserves the Byzantine arc diagram (*schemata*) illustrating :math:`\beta, \gamma, \delta` with upper arcs marked *hemiolios* (:math:`2:3`) and *epitritos* (:math:`3:4`) spanned beneath by *diplasios* (:math:`2:4`).
