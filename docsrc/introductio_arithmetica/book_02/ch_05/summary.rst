==================================================================================
Chapter 5: Composition and Resolution of Multiples from Superparticulars — Summary
==================================================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0250
   :page: p. 234

Overview
--------

Nicomachus formulates the fundamental multiplicative law connecting the :term:`multiple <pollaplasios>` and :term:`superparticular <epimorios>` genera:

1. **Resolution of the Double (II.5.1–2)**: The first two superparticulars—the :term:`sesquialter <hemiolios>` (:math:`3:2`) and the :term:`sesquitertian <epitritos>` (:math:`4:3`)—compose to produce the first multiple, the **double** (:math:`2:1`), as shown by :math:`2, 3, 4` and :math:`3, 4, 6`; conversely, every double resolves into a sesquialter and a sesquitertian.
2. **Inductive Ladder of Multiples (II.5.3–5)**: Composing the :math:`k`-fold multiple with the :math:`k`-th superparticular :math:`(k+1):k` always yields the :math:`(k+1)`-fold multiple: double :math:`\times` sesquialter (:math:`2 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 3`, e.g., :math:`6, 9, 12, 18`), triple :math:`\times` sesquitertian (:math:`3 \cdot \frac{4}{3} = 4`), quadruple :math:`\times` sesquiquartan (:math:`4 \cdot \frac{5}{4} = 5`), and so forth indefinitely.
