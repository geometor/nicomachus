Mathematical & Textual Notes
----------------------------

* **Potential vs. Actual Figuration**: Nicomachus systematically applies the Aristotelian distinction between *dynamis* (potentiality) and *energeia* (actuality) so that :math:`1` stands as the potential first term of every polygonal and solid series.
* **Manuscript Diagrams (Hoche pp. 89–90)**: Codex Gottingensis (*G*) displays alpha-pebble triangles `[1]` through `[7]` on p. 89 (`hoche_1866:0107`) and a circular-arc generation table aligning the natural series :math:`1 \dots 8` with triangles :math:`1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, 28, 36` in the left margin of p. 90 (`hoche_1866:0108`).
