Mathematical & Historical Notes
-------------------------------

* **Nicomachus's Square-Difference Identity (II.23.6)**: :person:`Boethius <boethius>` (*De Inst. Arith.* II.43) explicitly credits Nicomachus with discovering the identity :math:`b^2 - ac = d^2` (also mentioned in Nicomachus's *Enchiridion Harmonices* ch. 8, p. 251.15 Von Jan). Writing :math:`a = b - d` and :math:`c = b + d` yields :math:`(b - d)(b + d) = b^2 - d^2` immediately.
* **Ratio Ordering**: The observation that arithmetic proportions have larger ratios among smaller terms (:math:`2:1 > 3:2` in :math:`1, 2, 3`) while harmonic proportions reverse this (:math:`4:3 < 6:4` in :math:`3, 4, 6`) already appears in :person:`Archytas <archytas>` Fragment B2 (preserved by Porphyry, *In Ptol. Harm.* p. 267; cf. :cite:`dooge:1926`, p. 269 n. 4).
* **Geometric Realization**: Compare :g:`radius OR = A <three-classical-means-semicircle:OR>` on :g:`semicircle PR <three-classical-means-semicircle:SEMICIRC>` in ``geometor.nicomachus.construct_three_classical_means_model``.
