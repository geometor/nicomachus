Mathematical & Textual Notes
----------------------------

* **Alpha-Graphia (*Psēphophoria*)**: Nicomachus preserves the ancient Pythagorean practice of representing numbers by counters (*psēphoi*) arranged into geometric shapes; in Greek manuscripts these units are written with repeated alphas (`α`).
* **Six Directions of Space**: The enumeration of the six rectilinear directions around any solid echoes Plato (*Timaeus* 43b) and Aristotle (*Physics* IV.1, 208b12; *De Caelo* II.2).
