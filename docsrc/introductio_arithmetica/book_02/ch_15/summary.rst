Overview
--------

Nicomachus turns from pyramidal solids to prismatic and cubic solids generated by multiplying plane squares by a third dimension:

1. **Non-Pyramidal Solids (II.15.1)**: Introduces cubes, beams (*dokides*), bricks (*plinthides*), wedges (*sphēniskoi*), spherical numbers (*sphairikoi*), and parallelepipeds.
2. **Generation of Cubes (II.15.2)**: Multiplying each square $n^2$ ($1, 4, 9, 16, \dots$) by its own side $n$ yields the cubic series $1, 8, 27, 64, \dots$, with $1$ as the cube in potentiality.
3. **Polyhedral Anatomy (II.15.3)**: Contrasts the square ($1$ face, $4$ sides, $4$ angles) with the cube ($6$ square faces, $12$ equal edges, $8$ solid angles, each bounded by $3$ edges).
