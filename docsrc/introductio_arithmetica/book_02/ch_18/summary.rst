======================================================================================
Chapter 18: Pythagorean Dualities of Same (Odd/Square) & Other (Even/Oblong) — Summary
======================================================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0273
   :page: p. 257

Overview
--------

Nicomachus maps the metaphysical dualism of Plato's *Timaeus* and Philolaus's cosmology onto the arithmetic contrast between squares and heteromecics:

1. **Seed Principles (II.18.1)**: Unity/odd/:g:`squares <heteromecic-same-other:SAME>` embody the *Same* (bounded equality), while dyad/even/:g:`heteromecics <heteromecic-same-other:OTHER>` embody the *Other* (primary inequality).
2. **Self-Lengthed vs. Other-Lengthed (II.18.2–3)**: Re-contrasts *promecic* rectangles (side gap $\ge 2$) with *heteromecic* ($n(n+1)$), naming squares *idiomēkeis* / *tautomēkeis* ("self-lengthed").
3. **Plato & Philolaus (II.18.4)**: Links arithmetic duality directly to Plato's *Same/Other* and *Indivisible/Divisible* (*Timaeus* 35a) and Philolaus's *Limiting* and *Unlimited* (*perainonta* and *apeira*).
