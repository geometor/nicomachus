Archival Witnesses & Apparatus
------------------------------

* **English Witness**: D'Ooge (1926), pp. 257–259 (folios ``introductio_arithmetica:0273``–``0275``).
* **Greek Witness**: Hoche (1866), pp. 112–115 (folios ``hoche_1866:0130``–``0133``).
* **Boethius Parallel**: :person:`Boethius <boethius>`, *De Institutione Arithmetica* II.31–32 (:cite:`boethius:friedlein:1867`, pp. 120–122 Friedlein).

Philological & Textual Notes
----------------------------

* **Plato & Philolaus Citations (II.18.4)**: Nicomachus paraphrases :person:`Plato <plato>`, *Timaeus* 35a (:cite:`plato:timaeus:27d-36d`) on the mixture of *Same*, *Other*, *Indivisible*, and *Divisible* in the World-Soul, and quotes :person:`Philolaus <philolaus>` fr. B1–B2 (Diels-Kranz) in Doric (*ta eonta panta eimen ētoi apeira ē perainonta...*).
* **Manuscript Lacuna (Hoche p. 113)**: In II.18.2, Hoche prints ``τετράκις, καὶ οἱ παραπλήσιοι``, omitting the second factor ``η`` ("eight") supplied by the parallel passage in II.17.2 (``τετράκις η``).
