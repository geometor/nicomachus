Architectural Thesis
--------------------

Unit IV crowns the *Introductio Arithmetica* by uniting arithmetic, geometry, stereometry, and harmonics in the theory of means:

1. **The Three Classical Means & Semicircle Identity (II.21–27)**: Arithmetic (:math:`A = \frac{a+c}{2}`), Geometric (:math:`G = \sqrt{ac}`), and Harmonic (:math:`H = \frac{2ac}{a+c}`) means satisfy :math:`H \le G \le A` and the exact geometric mean law :math:`G^2 = A \cdot H`, constructed on :g:`semicircle PR <three-classical-means-semicircle:SEMICIRC>` via :g:`radius OR = A <three-classical-means-semicircle:OR>`, :g:`ordinate QS = G <three-classical-means-semicircle:QS>`, and :g:`projection QF = H <three-classical-means-semicircle:SF>`.
2. **The Complete Decad of Ten Proportions (II.28)**: Adding the three subcontrary means (4–6) and the four outer-difference means of :person:`Myonides <myonides>` and Euphranor (7–10) completes the Pythagorean Decad, culminating in :g:`Mean 10 (3, 5, 8) <three-classical-means-semicircle:MEAN10>` where :math:`a + b = c`.
3. **The Most Perfect Proportion 6 : 8 :: 9 : 12 (II.29)**: Built upon :person:`Philolaus's <philolaus>` Cube (:g:`6 faces <cube-most-perfect-proportion:FACES6>`, :g:`8 vertices <cube-most-perfect-proportion:VERTS8>`, :g:`12 edges <cube-most-perfect-proportion:EDGES12>`), the four-term :term:`teleiotatē analogia <teleiotate-analogia>` embeds Arithmetic (:math:`9`), Harmonic (:math:`8`), and Geometric (:math:`6 \cdot 12 = 8 \cdot 9 = 72`) symmetry alongside the :g:`octave 2:1 <cube-most-perfect-proportion:OCTAVE>`, :g:`fifth 3:2 <cube-most-perfect-proportion:FIFTH>`, :g:`fourth 4:3 <cube-most-perfect-proportion:FOURTH>`, and :g:`whole tone 9:8 <cube-most-perfect-proportion:TONE>`.
