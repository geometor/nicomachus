Mathematical & Textual Notes
----------------------------

* **Philosophical Context**: Nicomachus's definition of *stoicheion* echoes Aristotle (*Metaphysics* Δ.3, 1014a26) and Plato's *Timaeus* (48b), grounding arithmetic relations in the same bidirectional composition-and-resolution principle as cosmic elements.
* **Algorithmic Preview**: See :doc:`Chapter 2 <../ch_02/index>` for the universal three-subtraction reduction algorithm that carries any geometric triple back to :math:`(1, 1, 1)`.
