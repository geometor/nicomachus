Chapter 1: Element as Minimum & Equality as Principle of Relative Number — Summary
==================================================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0246
   :page: p. 230

Overview
--------

Nicomachus opens Book II by establishing that :term:`equality <isotes>` is not merely the origin from which all species of inequality are generated, but the true **element** (*stoicheion*) of relative quantity:

1. **Definition of an Element (II.1.1)**: Across grammar (letters), music (notes), and physics (fire, water, air, earth), an element is the minimal constituent out of which a composite arises and into which it is ultimately resolved.
2. **Absolute vs. Relative Elements (II.1.2)**: For absolute quantity, the :term:`monad <monas>` and dyad serve as foundational principles; for relative quantity, Book I showed how all five species of inequality unfold from equality via the three *prostagmata*. Book II now proves the converse: every inequality resolves back into equality alone.
