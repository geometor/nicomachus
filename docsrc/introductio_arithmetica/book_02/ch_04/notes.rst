Mathematical & Textual Notes
----------------------------

* **Algebraic Identity**: Moving one step right multiplies by :math:`m` and one step down multiplies by :math:`(m+1)/m`, so moving diagonally down-right multiplies by :math:`m \cdot \frac{m+1}{m} = m + 1`.
* **Textual Correction (Hoche p. 78)**: On folio ``hoche_1866:0096``, the bottom-right corner of the triple diagram is :math:`4^6 = 4096` (``͵δϟϛ``); earlier digital transcriptions occasionally misread this cell, which is verified directly on the Teubner scan.
