Historical & Philosophical Notes
--------------------------------

* **Authorship of the Ten Means**: As :person:`Iamblichus <iamblichus>` records (pp. 100.19, 113.16, 116.1 Pistelli), only the first three means (arithmetic, geometric, subcontrary/harmonic) go back to :person:`Pythagoras <pythagoras>` and his school (with :person:`Archytas <archytas>` and Hippasus renaming the third "harmonic"). :person:`Eudoxus of Cnidus <eudoxus>` added the 4th, 5th, and 6th means, while :person:`Myonides and Euphranor <myonides>` later supplied the 7th through 10th.
* **The *Theologumena Arithmeticae* Reference (II.22.1)**: When Nicomachus promises to discuss countless other manifestations of the Decad "in the proper place" (*en heterois*), he refers to his lost *Theologumena Arithmeticae* (preserved partly via Photius, *Bibl.* cod. 187, and the pseudo-Iamblichean compilation; cf. :cite:`dooge:1926`, p. 267 n. 4).
* **Co-Removal Cross-Reference**: See :doc:`Book I, Chapter 4 <../../book_01/ch_04/index>` for Nicomachus's full proof that arithmetic co-removes the remaining mathematical sciences without being co-removed by them.
