Philolaus of Croton
===================

- **Greek / Latin**: *Φιλόλαος ὁ Κροτωνιάτης*  |  **Role**: Pythagorean philosopher & harmonic theorist
- **Nicomachus Loci**: ``II.18, II.26.2 (p. 135), II.29 (pp. 144–147)``

Formulated the metaphysics of Limit and Unlimited; called the cube 'geometric harmony' (γεωμετρικὴ ἁρμονία) because its 6 faces, 8 vertices, and 12 edges embody the harmonic mean (II.26.2, II.29).
