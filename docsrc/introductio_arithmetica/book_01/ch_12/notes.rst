Editorial & Mathematical Notes
------------------------------

* **Odd Composites Only**: Just as Ch. 11 restricts primes to odd numbers, Ch. 12 exemplifies secondary and composite numbers solely with odd multiples (:math:`9, 15, 21, 25, 27, 33, 35, 39`), preserving the symmetrical tripartition between the three species of the even (Chs. 8–10) and the three species of the odd (Chs. 11–13).
