Editorial & Mathematical Notes
------------------------------

* **Exclusion of 2 (I.9.1–4)**: Because Nicomachus generates even-times odd numbers by doubling the odd numbers starting from :math:`3` (excluding the :term:`monad <monas>` as a number), :math:`2` is classified strictly with the even-times even (:math:`2^1`) rather than as :math:`2 \times 1`; contrast :person:`Euclid <euclid>`, *Elements* VII, Def. 9 (:cite:`euclid:elements:vii-ix`).
* **Arithmetic vs. Geometric Mean Contrast (I.9.6)**: The contrast between multiplicative pairings in Ch. 8 (:math:`2^j \cdot 2^{k-j} = 2^k`) and additive pairings in Ch. 9 (:math:`(4a+2) + (4b+2) = 2 \cdot (2a+2b+2)`) anticipates Book II's systematic study of arithmetic and geometric proportions (:term:`analogia`).
