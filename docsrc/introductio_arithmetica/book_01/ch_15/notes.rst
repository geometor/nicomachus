Literary & Mathematical Notes
-----------------------------

* **Hesiodic Quotation (I.15.1)**: The hexameter *"kykloterēs d' ophthalmos heeis enekeito metōpōi"* comes from Hesiod, *Theogony* 145, describing the Cyclopes.
* **Powers of Two as Minimally Deficient**: Notice that :math:`8 = 2^3` belongs to the evenly-even series (:math:`2^k`), whose proper parts :math:`1 + 2 + \dots + 2^{k-1} = 2^k - 1` always fall short of the whole by exactly :math:`1` (the "almost-perfect" defectiveness used in reverse in :doc:`Chapter 16 <../ch_16/index>`).
