Overview
--------

Completing the ontological ordering of the :term:`mathemata`, Nicomachus demonstrates why arithmetic also precedes :g:`music <quadrivium-tetrad:MUSIC>` and :g:`astronomy <quadrivium-tetrad:ASTRON>`:

1. **Priority over Music (I.5.1)**: Absolute quantity precedes relative quantity (just as *man* precedes *father*). Furthermore, all musical consonances—*diatessaron* (:math:`4:3`, :term:`epitritos <epitritos>`), *diapente* (:math:`3:2`, :term:`hemiolios <hemiolios>`), *diapason* (:math:`2:1`), twelfth (:math:`3:1`), and double-octave *di-diapason* (:math:`4:1`)—derive both their names and their ratios from pure number.
2. **Priority over Astronomy (I.5.2–3)**: Astronomy (*sphairikē*) follows geometry (since motion follows rest) and presupposes harmonic proportions in celestial revolutions, while planetary risings, settings, progressions, retrogressions, and occultations are governed by numerical periods. Arithmetic therefore stands first as "mother and nurse" (*mētēr kai tithēnē*) of all sciences.
