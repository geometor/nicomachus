Chapter 17: Relative Quantity, Equality & the Five Species of Inequality — Notes
================================================================================

Archival Witnesses & Apparatus
------------------------------

* **Greek Witness**: Hoche (1866), pp. 44–46 (folios ``hoche_1866:0062``–``0064``).
* **Boethius Parallel**: :person:`Boethius <boethius>`, *De Institutione Arithmetica* I.21–22 (:cite:`boethius:friedlein:1867`, pp. 45–47 Friedlein).

Architectural & Mathematical Notes
----------------------------------

* **Completeness of the Fivefold Division**: Every reduced rational ratio :math:`a : b` (:math:`a > b`, :math:`\gcd(a, b) = 1`) written via Euclidean division :math:`a = m \cdot b + r` (:math:`m \ge 1`, :math:`0 \le r < b`) falls into exactly one of Nicomachus's five species: Multiple (:math:`m \ge 2, r = 0`), Superparticular (:math:`m = 1, r = 1`), Superpartient (:math:`m = 1, r \ge 2`), Multiple Superparticular (:math:`m \ge 2, r = 1`), or Multiple Superpartient (:math:`m \ge 2, r \ge 2`), as verified in ``geometor.nicomachus.classify_nicomachean_ratio``.
* **Comparison with Theon of Smyrna**: :person:`Theon of Smyrna <theon-of-smyrna>` (:cite:`theon:hiller:1878`, pp. 74–76) preserves both a musical classification (with a class of "neither," *hoi oudeteroi*, for :math:`9:8` and :math:`256:243`) and an arithmetical classification agreeing with Nicomachus's fivefold scheme.
* **Homogeneous Magnitudes**: As D'Ooge (p. 212 n. 2) observes, Nicomachus presupposes :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` requirement (*Elements* V Def. 4) that only homogeneous quantities (length to length, weight to weight, number to number) stand in ratio.
