Archival Witnesses & Apparatus
------------------------------

* **English Witness**: D'Ooge (1926), pp. 189–190 (folios ``introductio_arithmetica:0205``–``0206``).
* **Greek Witness**: Hoche (1866), pp. 12–13 (folios ``hoche_1866:0030``–``0031``).
* **Boethius Parallel**: :person:`Boethius <boethius>`, *De Institutione Arithmetica* I.2 (:cite:`boethius:friedlein:1867`, pp. 12–13 Friedlein).

Philosophical & Textual Notes
-----------------------------

* **Philolaus on Harmony of Opposites (I.6.3)**: The doctrine that harmony arises exclusively from existent, distinct, and commensurable elements echoes :person:`Philolaus <philolaus>` DK 44 B6 (as D'Ooge notes, p. 190 n. 1, comparing :cite:`plato:timaeus:27d-36d` and :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>`).
* **Demiurgic Paradigm (I.6.1)**: The characterization of number as *logos technikos* and *procharagma* in the Creator's intellect synthesizes Neopythagorean arithmetic with Middle Platonic theology of the Ideas as thoughts of God.
