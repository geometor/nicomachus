Editorial & Mathematical Notes
------------------------------

* **Algebraic Form**: A :term:`multiple superparticular <pollaplasiepimorios>` ratio has the exact form :math:`(nk + 1) : k = n + \frac{1}{k}` for integers :math:`n \ge 2` and :math:`k \ge 2`. Its root-forms (*pythmenes*) are always relatively prime (:math:`\gcd(nk + 1, k) = 1`), and multiplying both terms by :math:`m = 2, 3, \dots` fills out each species sequence.
