Chapter 22: The Multiple Superparticular Species — Summary

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0238

Overview
--------

Nicomachus defines and tabulates the first compound species of relative inequality, the :g:`multiple superparticular <five-species-ratios:SP4>` (*pollaplasiepimorios*), together with its reciprocal submultiple superparticular:

1. **Definition & General Form (I.22.1–2)**: The greater term contains the lesser more than once *plus* one aliquot part of the lesser (:math:`n + \frac{1}{k}` with :math:`n \ge 2, k \ge 2`). Its root-forms combine the multiple prefix (*double*, *triple*, *quadruple*, ...) with the superparticular suffix (*sesquialter*, *sesquitertian*, *sesquiquartan*, ...), e.g., double sesquialter (:math:`5:2`), double sesquitertian (:math:`7:3`), double sesquiquartan (:math:`9:4`).
2. **Reciprocal Series & Parallel Tables (I.22.3–6)**: Every species pairs with a ``sub-`` reciprocal (*subdouble sesquialter*, etc.). Comparing successive rows of the canonical multiplication table (from Ch. 19) against multiples of :math:`1, 2, 3, \dots` displays the entire double, triple, quadruple, and higher multiple superparticular families in exact numerical order.
