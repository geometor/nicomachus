Philosophical Notes
-------------------

* **Asymmetric Co-Removal (*Synanairein*)**: Nicomachus's criterion adapts Aristotle's *Metaphysics* 1019a1 and 1059b39 (*synanaireitai tois genesei ta eidē*) together with *Topics* II.4 and VI.6 (*synepiberei* / implication). In the thirteenth century, :person:`Thomas Aquinas <aquinas>` analyzes this exact Boethian-Nicomachean hierarchy in *Super Boethium De Trinitate* q. 5 (:cite:`aquinas:in-boethium-trinitate`).
* **Geometric Polyhedra**: Notice that Nicomachus cites the *octahedron* (8 faces) and *icosahedron* (20 faces) alongside plane *triangle* and *quadrilateral* to show that even stereometry depends on cardinal numbers.
