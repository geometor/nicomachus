Mathematical & Historical Notes
-------------------------------

* **Submultiple Nomenclature**: Although Nicomachus defines *hypodiplasios*, *hypotriplasios*, and *hypotetra-plasios* here, in practice (D'Ooge p. 214 n. 2) he frequently refers to these reciprocal relations simply as "half," "third," and "fourth."
* **Root Sequence**: Comparing the natural integers :math:`1, 2, 3, 4, \dots` directly against the monad :math:`1` produces the root terms (*pythmenes*) of all multiple ratios (:math:`2:1, 3:1, 4:1, \dots`), which form the main spine of the Equality Unfolding Tree in :doc:`Chapter 23 <../ch_23/index>`.
