========================================================
Chapter 7: Definition of Number, Even, and Odd — Summary
========================================================

.. folio:: introductio_arithmetica:0206
   :page: p. 190

Overview
--------

Nicomachus establishes the threefold definition of :term:`number <arithmos>` and four complementary criteria distinguishing **even** from **odd**:

1. **Threefold Definition of Number (I.7.1)**: Number (*arithmos*) is (a) bounded multitude (*plēthos hōrismenon*), (b) an assembly of monads (*monadōn systēma*), or (c) a flow of quantity composed of monads (*posotētos chyma*).
2. **Popular Bisection Criterion (I.7.2)**: Even numbers split into two equal parts without a mediating :term:`monad <monas>`; odd numbers cannot be halved because unity intervenes.
3. **Pythagorean Extremal Criterion (I.7.3)**: Even numbers simultaneously split into parts greatest in size (:math:`n/2`) and smallest in count (:math:`2`), illustrating the inverse opposition of magnitude (*pēlikotēs*) and multitude (*posotēs*).
4. **Purity vs. Mixture Under Binary Partition (I.7.4)**: Any two-part partition of an even number (> 2) yields either two evens or two odds (*akrata*, unmixed), whereas every two-part partition of an odd number yields one even and one odd together.
5. **Mutual Unit-Shift Criterion (I.7.5)**: Odd and even alternate by :math:`\pm 1` along the natural sequence.
