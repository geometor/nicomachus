Editorial & Mathematical Notes
------------------------------

* **Algebraic Proof of the Three *Prostagma* Rules**: Starting from a geometric progression :math:`(a, b, c) = (x^2, xy, y^2)` in ratio :math:`y : x`:

  * In **direct** order :math:`(x^2, xy, y^2)`, the three rules produce :math:`(x^2,\; x(x+y),\; (x+y)^2)`, which is a geometric progression with ratio :math:`\frac{x+y}{x} = 1 + \frac{y}{x}`. Starting from :math:`(1, 1, 1)` (:math:`x=y=1`) steps :math:`\frac{y}{x} = 1 \to 2 \to 3 \to 4 \dots`, generating all multiples :math:`n : 1`.
  * In **reversed** order (*anastrophe*) :math:`(y^2, xy, x^2)`, the three rules produce :math:`(y^2,\; y(y+x),\; (y+x)^2)`, with ratio :math:`\frac{x+y}{y} = 1 + \frac{x}{y}`. Applying this to reversed multiples :math:`(n^2, n, 1)` gives :math:`1 + \frac{1}{n}` (all superparticulars); applying it to reversed superparticulars gives :math:`1 + \frac{n}{n+1}` (superpartients), while direct superparticulars give :math:`2 + \frac{1}{n}` (double superparticulars).
