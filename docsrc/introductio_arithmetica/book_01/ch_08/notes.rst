Editorial & Mathematical Notes
------------------------------

* **Nicomachus vs. Euclid on Even-Times Even (I.8.4–8)**: In *Elements* VII, Def. 8 (:cite:`euclid:elements:vii-ix`), :person:`Euclid <euclid>` defines an *even-times even* number broadly as any number measured by an even number according to an even number (allowing e.g. :math:`24 = 4 \times 6`, which is also *even-times odd*). Nicomachus and :person:`Iamblichus <iamblichus>` (:cite:`iamblichus:pistelli:1894`, pp. 26–29) restrict *artiakis artios* strictly to pure powers of two :math:`2^k` (:math:`k \ge 1`), ensuring the three classes of even numbers partition the even integers disjointly.
* **Geometric Series Lemma (I.8.12–13)**: Nicomachus's inductive observation that :math:`\sum_{j=0}^{k-1} 2^j = 2^k - 1` provides the exact arithmetic bridge to :cite:`euclid:elements:vii-ix` IX.35–36 and Book I, Ch. 16.
