geometor.nicomachus.ten_means_and_cube¶

Ten Nicomachean Proportions & the Cube Harmony 6 : 8 :: 9 : 12 (Book II, Chs. 21–29).

Formalizes the culminating chapters of Nicomachus of Gerasa’s Introduction to Arithmetic:

  • All Ten Means (δέκα μεσότητες, II.21–28):

    • The three classical archetypes from Pythagoras, Archytas, Plato, and Aristotle: 1. Arithmetic, 2. Geometric, 3. Harmonic.

    • The three subcontrary proportions (ὑπεναντίαι): 4. Subcontrary to Harmonic, 5. First Subcontrary to Geometric, 6. Second Subcontrary to Geometric.

    • The four later Neo-Pythagorean proportions (τέσσαρες ἕτεραι, Myonides & Euphranor): 7th, 8th, 9th, and 10th — where the 10th Mean (\((z - x)/(z - m) = m/x\)) reduces for \(m \ne x\) to the Fibonacci additive law \(x + m = z\) (canonical example \(3, 5, 8\)).

  • Semicircle Construction of the Three Classical Means (cf. Pappus Synagoge III): Exact Model construction proving visually \(A \ge G \ge H\) and \(A \cdot H = G^2\).

  • The Most Perfect Proportion (*Τελειοτάτη Ἀναλογία*, II.29): \(6 : 8 :: 9 : 12\), uniting the topology of the Cube (6 faces, 8 vertices, 12 edges — Philolaus’s “geometric harmony”), Arithmetic (9), Harmonic (8), and Geometric (\(6 \cdot 12 = 8 \cdot 9 = 72\)) symmetry, projective harmonic cross-ratio \((6, 12; 8, 0) = -1\), and all Pythagorean musical consonances.

geometor.nicomachus.ten_means_and_cube.compute_nicomachean_mean(index: int, x: int | sympy.Expr, z: int | sympy.Expr) → sympy.Expr¶

Compute the exact index-th Nicomachean mean m between 0 < x < z (II.23–28).

Note on the 10th Mean: its defining equation \((z - x)/(z - m) = m/x\) factors as \((m - x)(m - (z - x)) = 0\); hence a strict interior mean \(x < m < z\) exists if and only if the diorismos \(z > 2x\) holds, in which case \(m = z - x\) (\(x + m = z\)).

Parameters:

  • index – Nicomachean proportion ordinal 1..10.

  • x – Lesser positive extreme (concrete number or positive symbolic expression).

  • z – Greater positive extreme satisfying z > x (and z > 2*x when index == 10 for concrete numerical inputs).

Returns:

Exact simplified sympy.Expr for m.

Raises:

ValueError – If index is outside 1..10 or concrete bounds violate 0 < x < z (or z <= 2*x for index == 10).

Build an exact semicircle Model of Arithmetic, Geometric, and Harmonic means.

Defaults to a_len = 6, c_len = 12 (the Cube extremes of Book II, Ch. 29), where OS = 9 (Arithmetic), QS = 6*sqrt(2) (Geometric), and SF = 8 (Harmonic), all in \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})\) with Q(MeanA) * Q(MeanH) == Q(MeanG)**2 == 5184.

Parameters:

  • a_len – First positive segment length a.

  • c_len – Second positive segment length c.

Returns:

Verified exact symbolic Model.

Raises:

ValueError – If concrete inputs are non-positive.

geometor.nicomachus.ten_means_and_cube.construct_cube_proportion_model() → CubeHarmonicResult¶

Build and verify Nicomachus’s Teleiotatē Analogia 6 : 8 :: 9 : 12 (Book II, Ch. 29).

Constructs T9(9, 0) by Euclidean bisection and T8(8, 0) by a pure straightedge complete quadrilateral from Z(0, 0), T6(6, 0), T12(12, 0), then verifies:

  • Topological Cube witness: 6 faces, 8 vertices, 12 edges (Euler 6 + 8 - 12 = 2).

  • Projective harmonic range (T6, T12; T8, Z) == -1.

  • Musical consonances: octave 12:6 = 2:1, fifth 9:6 = 12:8 = 3:2, fourth 8:6 = 12:9 = 4:3, and whole tone 9:8.

Returns:

Verified CubeHarmonicResult.
